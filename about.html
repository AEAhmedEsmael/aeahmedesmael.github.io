About Me

Cloud Solutions Architect

As an IT expert with over 14 years of hands-on experience, I specialize in building resilient, secure, and scalable IT infrastructures across on-premises, hybrid, and cloud environments. My work includes enterprise architecture, cybersecurity operations, and large-scale transformation initiatives.

My career has grown from system administration to leading enterprise-scale transformations in data centers, cloud platforms, and security operations. I’ve led teams managing 1000+ servers across 400+ sites, implemented secure backup strategies for critical workloads, and driven cloud adoption while maintaining compliance and business continuity.
